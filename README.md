# Emmanuel-Healthcare-Analytics-SQL-KPI
SQL analysis of patient healthcare data to identify billing trends, treatment durations, and medical risk categories
Project Overview
I developed a series of SQL queries to extract Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) from a healthcare dataset containing over 500 records. The goal was to provide actionable insights into patient demographics, financial billing, and medical risk categories.  Key Technical Features Demonstrated:
Window Functions: Utilized DENSE_RANK() and RANK() to identify patient age trends and medication frequency.
Data Segmentation: Created a custom Risk Category logic using CASE statements to classify patients into High, Medium, or Low risk based on medical conditions.
Duration Analysis: Applied DATEDIFF() to calculate the average length of stay and time taken to achieve "Normal" test results.
Financial Modeling: Aggregated total and average billing amounts by medical condition and insurance provider to identify revenue trends.  Business Impact: Identified the most preferred insurance providers and hospitals to optimize partnership focus. 
Automated the identification of universal blood donors (O-) and receivers (AB+) for emergency preparedness.
